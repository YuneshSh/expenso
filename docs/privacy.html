Expenso Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Expenso (“the Extension”) is a Chrome extension for personal expense and budget tracking. This policy explains what data is handled and how.

1. Data we handle

Expenso stores the following information that you enter while using the Extension:

  • Expense categories (names and colors)
  • Monthly income and budget amounts
  • Category budget allocations
  • Expense entries (amount, date, optional notes, category)

This is financial tracking information you provide. The Extension does not ask for your name, email address, passwords, payment card numbers, or location.

2. Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome’s chrome.storage.local API. Data is tied to the Chrome profile where the Extension is installed.

We do not operate a backend server for Expenso, and we do not transmit your expense data to our servers.

3. Backup export / import

You may optionally export a JSON backup file and import it on another Chrome profile. That file is created and read only on your device at your request. We do not receive a copy.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Expenso data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising, credit scoring, or lending decisions.

5. Permissions

Expenso requests the storage permission solely to save and read the local data described above so the Extension can function offline.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete expenses/categories in the Extension, clear extension storage, or uninstall the Extension. Uninstalling Expenso removes its local data for that Chrome profile.

7. Children’s privacy

Expenso is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Extension after updates means you accept the revised policy.
